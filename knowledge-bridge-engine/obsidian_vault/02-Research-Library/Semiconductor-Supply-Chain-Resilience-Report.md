## Abstract

This report assesses the resilience of advanced-node semiconductor supply chains under
the current United States export-control regime and the corresponding Chinese
localization push. We map chokepoints across lithography equipment, advanced packaging,
and high-bandwidth memory, and quantify substitution timelines for each tier.

## Key Findings

- Advanced packaging capacity outside Taiwan grew 14 per cent in 2025 but remains
  insufficient to absorb a one-quarter disruption scenario.
- Chinese domestic foundries closed roughly half the logic-node gap projected in 2023,
  driven by deep-ultraviolet multi-patterning rather than extreme-ultraviolet access.
- European and Canadian fab incentive programs are oversubscribed yet under-built;
  announced capacity converts to wafers at only 60 per cent of the headline rate.

## Strategic Implications

Firms with single-region dependence on advanced packaging face the largest tail risk;
dual-sourcing premiums of 8 per cent are economically justified for AI accelerator
supply chains through 2028.
