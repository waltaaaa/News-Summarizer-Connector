## Summary

A Nature poll reveals that many scientists feel pressure to adopt or experiment with AI tools despite uncertainty about their utility, driven by fear of missing out.

## Key Takeaway

FOMO-driven AI adoption in research communities can lead to inefficient tool deployment without sufficient validation of benefits.
