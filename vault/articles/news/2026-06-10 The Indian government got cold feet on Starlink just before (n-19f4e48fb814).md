India's government reversed its support for SpaceX's Starlink satellite internet service just before SpaceX pursued an IPO, citing regulatory concerns.

## Key Takeaway

Geopolitical and regulatory pressures can derail satellite internet expansion in major markets even as companies pursue public offerings.
